===== stop / start : les données du conteneur sont conservées =====
$ ./kafka-topics.sh --bootstrap-server kafka:9092 --create --topic ticketflow.persistance.test --partitions 1 --replication-factor 1
WARNING: Due to limitations in metric names, topics with a period ('.') or underscore ('_') could collide. To avoid issues it is best to use either, but not both.
Created topic ticketflow.persistance.test.

$ docker compose stop

$ docker compose start
